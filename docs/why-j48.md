# Why "J48"?

The J48 Programmer is named for the Pratt & Whitney J48 turbojet, the engine in the Grumman F9F Cougar.

In the mid-1950s, LeRoy Alonzo Morgan was a Navy aviation mechanic with Fighter Squadron 214 (VF-214, later VA-214, the "Volunteers") at NAS Moffett Field, California. The squadron flew FJ-3 Furies, then F9F-8 Cougars powered by the J48, and made Western Pacific cruises aboard USS *Yorktown* in 1956 and USS *Hornet* in 1958. LeRoy was plane captain for the squadron commander's aircraft and later supervised the squadron's airframe and powerplant mechanics. He also worked on Panthers, Crusaders and AJ Savages, and later served in the Naval Reserve at El Centro.

This project's inspiration is Afterburner. The J48 in the Cougar had no afterburner. The host software is called Wingman because it flies alongside.
